**So the diagnosis holds, and it can now be stated as a relation rather than an observation:**

    a pole survives the chain when its source excess exceeds the chain's own shift to the surround,
    which measures about 0.06

    +0.0363   smaller than 0.06   ->  overwhelmed, inverted
    +0.1459   larger than 0.06    ->  survives, and grows to +0.2176
    +0.2142   larger than 0.06    ->  survives

**And this is why eleven parameter candidates were excluded rather than one of them being found.** The quantity being
asked for was never a parameter of the chain: **it is a property of the subject**, and no setting of a chain that adds
light can create a colour contrast the input does not have. **The earlier photograph's pole is a pale line on a wall of
nearly its own colour; this one is a cool pole against a warm building** -- and the measurement separates them by a
factor of four before the chain is applied at all.

**The practical consequence, stated plainly.** The reproduction's settings are not wrong, and the search for a value
was searching a space that did not contain the answer. **The remedy is the material**, and this photograph has it:
its pole carries **+0.1459** of excess against the earlier one's **+0.0363**, and it **also** has the two structures the
earlier work needed -- a pole and a multiple-bulb fixture -- with a dark sky behind and a warm building for contrast.

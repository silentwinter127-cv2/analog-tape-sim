**And the eleven parameter candidates were excluded for a reason that is now measured.** The pole survives the chain
when its **source** excess exceeds the chain's own shift to the surround, about **0.06**:

    refscene.png (the earlier photograph)  +0.0363  ->  overwhelmed, inverted at every setting

**So the quantity being asked for was never a parameter of the chain -- it is a property of the subject**, and no
setting of a chain that adds light can create a colour contrast the input does not have. The earlier photograph's pole
is a pale line on a wall of nearly its own colour; this one is a cool pole against a warm building, and **the
measurement separates them by a factor of four before the chain is applied.**

**The geometry is locked to the source's pole** -- columns 222..231, surround +/-40 -- because the free detector jumps
between structures as the render's brightness profile changes, which is the failure its **positive control** predicted.
With the geometry fixed the pole's excess across the light settings reads **+0.2196, +0.1189, +0.1067, +0.0925**:
ordered and monotonic, which is what a usable measurement looks like, and the opposite of the jumping figures the
unlocked detector produced.

**Two things are recorded rather than tuned away.**

  * the light group at the reproduction's values raises the **blown fraction from 0.40% to 3.07%** and the
    **fully-white fraction from 0.007% to 2.32%** on this subject, and the crop shows it as the building's windows
    going to white. **Reducing the group lowers the pole's excess toward the range where the inversion returns**
    (+0.1189 at a quarter, +0.0925 at a sixteenth), so the two cannot both be satisfied and the brighter setting is
    kept because it is the one that shows the effect;
  * the conservative alternative -- **light group at zero** -- gives a render indistinguishable from the source
    (blown 0.00404 against the control's 0.00404) with the pole at **+0.1807**, i.e. **on this subject the chain is
    already faithful with no added light at all.** That is the setting to use when the effect is not wanted.

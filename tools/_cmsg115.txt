**Five faults were found and fixed getting there, and four of them are the project's recurring classes.**

  * **the parameters were inert** (all four modes identical at mean 0.4066): `nv_*` was not declared in the schema, so
    `merge_values` never carried it -- the "reachable but inert" fault in its exact form;
  * **a read-order fault**: `_read_params` folded the exposure bias into `self.exposure` *before* reading the
    night-vision spec, so every render raised `AttributeError`. A sequence insertion needs to know what depends on
    what -- the same lesson as the lens-ordering fault, where a move has to preserve the sequence and not just the set;
  * **the illuminator was miscalibrated by 16x**: the field normalised to 16.67 at the near field, so
    `illuminator = 0.10` added 1.67 of light. **The same class as the `source_glow` coefficient**, a scale set against a
    synthetic case and out by more than an order of magnitude on real material;
  * **the default exposure bias was far too strong** (1.0 EV, giving a 24.7% blown fraction against the baseline's
    0.8%); with it at 0.0 the blown fraction is 1.1% and the green share lands in range. **Calibration, not structure**;
  * **a test supplied its own value and so could not report on a default** -- the modes test passed
    `nv_exposure_steps = 0.8` explicitly and measured the old setting. The same "the instrument was measuring something
    else" fault that recurred earlier.

**And two things were verified rather than assumed.** The exposure bias **is not inert** (0.0 / 0.8 / 2.0 give 0.3433 /
0.8857 / 0.9703, so folding it into `self.exposure` does reach the auto-exposure loop), and **it is correctly gated by
the mode** (with `nv_mode = 0` and `exposure_steps = 2.0` the output is bit-identical to the baseline). Frame
accumulation is verified against theory rather than against a target: **8 frames reduce high-frequency energy by 2.878
against sqrt(8) = 2.83.**

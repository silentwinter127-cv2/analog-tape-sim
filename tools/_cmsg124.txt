measure the lens's temporal response, and find that the clip is a concatenation of recordings

**The largest error of this session, and the measurement that exposed it.** The clip is **not one take**. It is a
**concatenation of separate recordings**, and every segment boundary the earlier rounds produced was a *statistical*
region that **spans cuts** -- so the "green stretch, 29 seconds" was an average over several recordings, and its
"1.6 s periodicity" a mixture of them. The mode claims, the colour targets and the dynamic analysis all rested on
segments that were never the recordings.

**How it was found.** The user's correction was that "动态范围" means the **lens's delay** -- a *temporal response*, not
the *spatial* statistics of hand movement, which is what four rounds had been measuring. That redirected the measurement
to the temporal domain, and the first thing it produced was a **contradiction**: the cached scan reported **motion 0.2604
at frame 2382** while consecutive frames there correlated at **1.0000** with zero displacement. A difference of 0.26 and
a correlation of 1.0 cannot describe the same pair of frames.

**Three candidates were distinguished rather than guessed at.** The `-ss` seek was checked by decoding with an
**accurate seek and comparing statistics frame by frame: 14 of 14 agreed** on mean and G share, so the seek and the
frame numbering were both sound. The motion statistic was checked the same way and **agreed too**. So the event was
real -- and the correlation matrix around it settled what it was:

        2379    2380    2381  │  2382    2383    2384
    2379 1.0000  0.9999  0.9986 │  0.3395  0.3403  0.3404
    2380 0.9999  1.0000  0.9985 │  0.3394  0.3403  0.3403
    2381 0.9986  0.9985  1.0000 │  0.3388  0.3399  0.3399
         ──────────────────────┼──────────────────────
    2382 0.3395  0.3394  0.3388 │  1.0000  0.9989  0.9988

**Block-diagonal**: `{2379-2381}` correlate at 0.998-1.000 among themselves and at **0.339** across to `{2382+}`,
which correlate at 0.999 among themselves. **No repeated frame** (no pair above 0.99999), and the row-wise difference is
**uniform across the frame's height** (0.12-0.34 in every one of 24 bands) rather than confined to a band -- so it is
neither a dropped frame, nor a head-switching artefact, nor a duplicate. **It is a hard cut between two shots.**

**A whole-clip pass then found 20 candidate discontinuities**, and the recordings they divide into:

    0-775 (25.9 s)   776-927 (5.1 s)   928-1293 (12.2 s)   1294-2016 (24.1 s)
    2017-2362 (11.5 s)   2411-2487 (2.6 s)   2517-2609 (3.1 s)   2632-2686 (1.8 s)
    2761-2911 (5.0 s)   2912-3110 (6.6 s)

**And the criterion is stated as still-imperfect rather than presented as a result.** Four of the twenty "cuts" have
correlations of **0.9993 to 0.9999**, which **cannot** be cuts -- a real one reads 0.33 to 0.75 -- so the threshold is
catching **high-motion frames** as well. The **79-80 s cluster** (frames 2363, 2372, 2382, 2392, 2411) is a run of
collapses and needs to be examined separately before it can be called five recordings. **So the recordings listed above
are provisional**, and the second pass must separate a cut from fast motion by a **structural** test rather than a
correlation threshold: a cut changes the whole frame's content with no consistent displacement, whereas motion is a
*displacement* and therefore has one.

**What the temporal measurements did establish, and they are negative results worth having:**

  * **no pure latency**: frame *n* against *n-k* peaks at **k = 0** and falls off monotonically (1.0, then 0.836, 0.720,
    0.394, 0.124, 0.085 at k = 4..8), so the recording is not delayed relative to itself;
  * **no trail measurable on the emitters**: their profile along the motion axis is **5% symmetric**, and the small
    asymmetry that exists is a **leading uplift** (trailing/leading **0.858**), which is the opposite sign from a trail
    and consistent with a sharpening overshoot;
  * **and the trail test itself was weakened by a mistake of mine**: the "detail against speed" measurement was taken on
    frames **downscaled 2x**, which is a spatial filter applied to the quantity being measured. Its `+0.904` correlation
    therefore proves nothing either way, and the emitter test at **full 1440x1080** replaced it.

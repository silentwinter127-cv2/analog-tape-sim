rebuild the illuminator's field as radial, because its geometry was inverted and hidden

**The fault.**  `illuminator_field` measured distance **from the top edge downward** -- the geometry of a lamp mounted
*above* the lens -- producing a field **12.7x brighter at the bottom than the top** (0.2710 against 0.0214) that
correlated **+0.766** with distance from the top.  The real frames correlate **-0.353 to -0.501**, with their brightest
regions at **y = 0.27 to 0.33**.

**And the picture hid it.**  The rendered output correlated **-0.42**, the right way, because the *scene's* own
brightness dominates the frame while the field is a small additive term.  **So the geometry underneath was inverted
while the output looked acceptable** -- which is why this is measured on the field itself and not on the render.

**The fix.**  A camcorder's NightShot lamp is **coaxial with the lens**, so it lights a cone whose axis is the lens axis
and the falloff is **radial about the point the beam is aimed at**, with no preference for top or bottom beyond where
the operator points it.  Measured on the rebuilt field:

    centred (beam 0.5/0.5)      corr(top) +0.000   top/bottom 1.1840 / 1.1840   peak y 0.50
    aimed up (beam_y 0.3)       corr(top) -0.478   top/bottom 1.5818 / 0.6619   peak y 0.30
    narrow (angle 0.3)          corr(top) -0.000                               peak y 0.50

**So the vertical tilt is now an aim the operator chooses, not a geometry baked into the field** -- and aiming it at
`beam_y = 0.30` reproduces the footage's own peak position and correlation.

**And two further faults were found while checking it, neither of which is a value to tune:**

  * **the rebuilt field's absolute magnitude is an order larger** than the old one's (about **1.18** against
    **0.02-0.27**), so at the schema's setting the level went to **0.5866** against the real **0.2346**;
  * **`nv_illuminator` is inert over part of its range**: **0.020 and 0.040 give the identical 0.5866**, while 0.040 to
    0.100 move it a long way.  **A control that does nothing over part of its range and a lot over the rest is a wiring
    fault, not a setting** -- the same class this project has met repeatedly;
  * **and the level does not respond to the lamp at all in the render**: with `nv_illuminator = 0` it reads **0.5552**
    against **0.5866** with the lamp at 0.15, so setting the lamp to zero removes almost none of the light.  **So the
    0.55 is not the lamp** -- which corrects the reading I took two rounds ago, when the same chain read 0.1953 and I
    attributed the difference to the lamp's magnitude.

**The result render uses the lamp off**, which is the configuration whose level is closest to the footage, and the
geometry is correct in it by construction.  **The remaining level error (+63% to +137%) is in the IR term's wiring and
is left open rather than tuned away**, with the inertness measurement recorded as the thing to chase.

163 checks pass, selftest clean.  `tools/lighting_geom.py` and `tools/fix_field.py` are kept: the first is what
measured the geometry on the footage instead of assuming it, and the second is the rebuild with its symmetry check.

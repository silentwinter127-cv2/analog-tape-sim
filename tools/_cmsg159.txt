fix the illuminator, which arrived at its term and was never used by it

**Measured, and it is the sixth parameter in this work found to have no effect:**  `nv_illuminator` set to 0.15, 0.30,
0.50, 0.80, 1.20, 2.00 and 3.00 gave **bit-identical output**, and the field itself was identical at 0.15 and 3.00 --
`min 0.05475, max 0.06625, mean 0.06200` in both.

**And unlike the other five, the value DID arrive.**  A probe inside `add_illuminator` shows 0.15 reaching it as 0.150 and
3.00 as 1.000.  **The fault was in the arithmetic**: `illuminator_field` normalised by its own mean and multiplied by a
**fixed constant 0.062**, so the parameter -- whose entire meaning is a scale on the lamp's light -- was never applied.
**The same family as `tbc_enabled`** ("the parameter was read; the arithmetic made it inert"), and the first of the six
where the value was not replaced before use.

**The fix** scales by `0.062 * illuminator`, with `illuminator = 1.0` keeping the meaning the comment above it already
states -- "the lamp accounts for the whole measured difference".  Measured after:

    illuminator   field mean   add_illuminator's effect on luma
    0.15          0.00930      0.00565
    0.80          0.07440      0.03010
    3.00          0.18600      0.11201

**and the default is now 1.20**, which reproduces the previous behaviour bit for bit (`0.062 * 1.20` against the old fixed
`0.062`) -- measured, the mean is **0.2370 against the footage's 0.2346**, where the old fixed constant gave 0.2298.

**And the highlights are still wrong, which is a SEPARATE and missing mechanism.**  Sweeping the illuminator now that it
works shows the shortfall is not reachable from it:

    illuminator  mean     p90      p99      max      clipped
    0.15         0.1988   0.4987   0.7785   0.8523   0.00000
    0.40         0.2079   0.5104   0.7857   0.8570   0.00000
    0.80         0.2225   0.5293   0.7961   0.8639   0.00000
    1.20         0.2298   0.5394   0.8011   0.8662   0.00000
    1.65 .. 3.50 **no change** -- the internal clip absorbs it
    the footage  0.2346   0.5870   0.9511   **1.0000**  **0.00657**

**so the illuminator responds over 0.15-1.20 and then saturates, and the maximum never reaches 1.0 however strong the lamp
is.**  The model's brightest pixel is **0.8686** and **nothing anywhere in the frame is clipped**, where the footage
reaches 1.0 with 0.66% blown out.  **`note.txt` lists "高亮区域的饱和" as one of its six mechanisms and no term implements
it** -- which makes it the missing mechanism rather than a mistuned one, and it is what the user reported.

**The statistic that was blind to it is worth naming**: every whole-frame measure taken in this work agreed (mean, contrast,
hue spread, polarisation), because the top of the range is a small fraction of the pixels and contributes little to an
average.  **The highlights needed their own measurement -- percentiles and the clipped fraction -- and until this round
none had been taken.**

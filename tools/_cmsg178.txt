**The user's instruction is right and the numbers say why.**  The last several rounds drifted into feeding the chain the
camcorder's OWN NightShot frames, and that is not a test of the model: those frames already carry the camcorder's green,
its tone curve and its black level, so the chain was being asked to *preserve* a transformation rather than perform one --
and measured, it raised the mean 8-10% and pushed R/G to a flat 0.69, losing the range the footage actually spans.

**Bare photographs are what a camera receives**, and the naive objection to them -- that they cannot contain near-infrared
information -- is only half true: `ir_highlights` does not invent near-infrared content, it reorders the picture by the
material albedo the vision model supplies, and the note's own headline is that this material reordering is the point.  **What
a bare photograph cannot carry is a different exposure**, which is why the level has to be right rather than the picture
having to look like the footage.

record why the highlight colour stays invisible, and roll back four mechanisms that did not work

**The user's report was accurate and the cause is two inert parameters**, both of them the controls a person would
reach for. Neither is a weak mechanism; both are broken.

**`format.chroma_bandwidth` is the binding limit on the whole chroma path.** Measured on a hard edge with the
lateral aberration at full strength:

    format 0.75 / player 0.75    R-B fringe -0.007    colour at the edge 0.00782    the defaults
    format 2.00 / player 0.75    R-B fringe -2.801    colour at the edge 0.22159    <- 28x
    format 0.75 / player 2.00    R-B fringe -0.007    colour at the edge 0.00782    no effect

Widening the **recorder's** encoder takes the surviving fringe from 0.007 to 2.801 px and the colour present at
the edge by a factor of **28**. Widening the player's decoder alone does nothing, because the encoder has already
removed the detail. So the user raising `camera.chroma_aberration` to maximum was asking the lens for something
the **recording format** was discarding -- the honest lever is `chroma_bandwidth` in the format layer, where 0.75
MHz is VHS-grade, about 1.5 is S-VHS, and 2.0 is near-component.

**And `display.saturation` is inert.** Measured on a saturated red/blue chart, across the whole range:

    saturation 0.00  0.25  0.50  0.75  1.00
    mean |R-B| 0.37993 at every setting

Five settings, byte-identical output. **The single most obvious control for making colour more visible does
nothing at all**, which is half of why the effect could not be made to show -- the other half being the encoder
above it. This is the seventeenth inert parameter this project has found and it is not yet diagnosed.

**Four mechanisms were written and are rolled back.** Coma, astigmatism, spherical aberration and camera-side
distortion were added and each measured wrong:

    coma          corner spread 42.9 -> 56.3, so it acts, but the asymmetry fell (2.07 -> 1.39) instead of
                  growing, so the tail is not one-sided in the way the mechanism claims
    astigmatism   the axis ratio moved 0.53 -> 0.56 across the whole range: effectively inert
    spherical     the bright dot's halo moved 42.46 -> 42.78, 0.7%: inert
    distortion    the edge position was 196/204 at -0.5, 0.0 and +0.5: completely inert

**Three new inert parameters in one turn is the signal to stop, not to keep going.** The failure is not any single
bug: it is that `camera.py` has grown to the point where I cannot reliably land a mechanism in it in one pass. This
turn had four edits to one file and three of them produced no effect; the turn before had field curvature measuring
inert for two attempts before a correct measurement was found. **The file needs to be split before more is added to
it** -- the aberrations are a coherent group with a shared coordinate helper and they belong in their own module,
where an edit is small enough to verify and a fault is small enough to find.

So the repository is left green at 153 checks, the two inert parameters are recorded rather than half-fixed, and
the next step is a refactor rather than another mechanism.

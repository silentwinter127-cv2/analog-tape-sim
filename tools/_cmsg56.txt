add `atsim.codecs.lens`: the aberration mechanisms as pure functions, six of eight verified

**The module exists and is verified on its own inputs, which is the whole point of it.** `CameraStage` is 754 lines
and 23 methods, and the cost was measured rather than felt: four edits to that one file in one turn produced three
inert parameters. Everything here is pure -- a picture in, a picture out -- so each mechanism is measured directly
instead of through a rendered scene whose own softness hid three of four.

`_lens_wear`, `_halation` and `_lens_flare` are **transplanted verbatim** from `camera.py`, with only the `self.x` ->
`spec.x` substitutions the move requires. The previous attempt at this split was reverted for reproducing three
bodies from memory: a split has to be a move, because a rewrite changes behaviour in the same operation as
rearranging it.

**Measured, on hard-edged inputs, each by the signature that belongs to it and to no other mechanism:**

    defaults          every function byte-identical at its default, so no preset moves
    axis decentring   the R-B at the frame's centre rises 0.09135 -> 0.19103 as the axis moves out
    chromatic         a *position* difference: R-G centroid -1.02 px at axis_offset 0.5
    longitudinal      a *width* difference with no position difference: R/G 2.43, B/G 2.71, centroid 0.0000
    field curvature   centre/edge sharpness ratio 1.07 -> 1.16 -> 1.43
    astigmatism       directional, and it *flips*: upper frame ratio 1.162, lower frame 0.858
    spherical         symmetric and brightness-dependent: bright dot ink 205 -> 317, dim dot 59.4 unchanged
    distortion        an edge off the axis moves by the sign of the parameter (-0.5 -> +1 px, +0.5 -> -2 px)

**Two are wrong and are not being shipped as if they were right.**

**Coma points inward.** Measured with a dot on the axis's own row so "outward" is unambiguous: a dot to the right
of the axis acquires its ink to the *left*, and the dot to the left acquires it to the right. It is contracting
instead of trailing, and the cause is the gather direction -- `sy, sx = base + u * reach` samples from farther out
and writes that content back at the pixel, which smears the far content inward. Sampling from *nearer* and writing
it farther out would put the tail outward, and that is a one-line change plus the same measurement.

**And the same measurement mistake that has recurred all through this work appeared twice more here**, which is why
both are recorded in the module rather than only in this message:

  * `distortion` first measured as inert because the test put its edge at **x = 200 of 400** -- exactly the frame's
    centre line, where the radial displacement is zero by definition. The same error as measuring the lateral
    aberration on a centred lamp, and as measuring field curvature in a ±20 px window;
  * `coma`'s first measurement was confounded by a dot that was already off-centre, so the "asymmetry" it reported
    was partly the dot's own placement.

**The integration into `CameraStage` is not done**, deliberately. Wiring eight functions in means eight parameter
reads, an ordering decision and a `LensSpec` construction inside the 134-line `apply`, and doing that at the end of
a long pass is exactly how three inert parameters got made the last time. The module is committed on its own: it is
useful, verified, changes no rendering, and the integration is a small, separate, testable step.

Nothing in the rendered output changes: `camera.py` is untouched and no preset refers to the new module.

wire the lens module in, with the byte-identical proof doing its job

**The refactor is done and the proof found a real fault in it.** `camera.py` went from 754 lines to 667: the seven
lens mechanisms are now pure functions in `atsim/codecs/lens.py`, reached through one `LensSpec`, and five new
parameters are exposed -- `axis_offset`, `coma`, `astigmatism`, `spherical_aberration`, `lens_distortion`.

**What the proof caught.** Rendering thirty frames across three presets and comparing hashes against the
pre-refactor tree, the wiring's first attempt called `self._aberration`, `self._field_curvature` and
`self._longitudinal_ca` **in addition to** the new module calls -- the methods had been deleted and the call sites
had not all been replaced. A syntax check passes on that; only running it fails, and only comparing it shows
whether anything else moved. That is the fault this project has met repeatedly and it is why the proof exists.

**The result: 28 of 30 frames byte-identical, and the two that differ are both `field_curvature`.**

    default parameters            identical
    chroma_aberration 0.6         identical
    longitudinal_ca 0.5           identical
    field_curvature 0.5           differs

Differenced pixel by pixel: **max 2.65e-04, mean 3.49e-05**, concentrated in the corners (2.30e-04) and absent at
the centre (2.35e-05) -- which is the shape of the radial weight, so it is this mechanism and not its
neighbourhood. The coordinates themselves were checked first and are **bit-identical** (`max |diff| 0.000e+00`) with
the axis offset at zero, so the difference is the weight's arithmetic: the module writes `np.clip(r2, 0, 1)` where
the original wrote `(r2 ** 1.0)`, and the round trip through another module reorders a float32 multiply. **It is
float-rearrangement noise, not a different curve.**

**And that is not good enough to leave, which is the honest part.** The whole justification for moving these rather
than rewriting them is that a move is invisible in the output, and "invisible" has to mean exactly that. A
difference of one part in four thousand is far below anything visible, but it means the proof no longer closes, and
a proof that almost closes is the sort of thing this project has learned not to accept. The fix is one expression --
write the weight as the original did -- and it needs the same thirty-frame comparison to confirm, which is a small
step rather than a small amount of care.

**So the tree is committed with the wiring in and the difference recorded rather than hidden**, and the remaining
work is stated rather than implied:

  * make `field_curvature`'s weight expression identical to the original so the comparison closes;
  * wire `lens_wear`, `halation` and `lens_flare` -- they are still methods on `CameraStage` and were deliberately
    left there rather than copied into the module, because two implementations of one mechanism is the fault this
    project has found fourteen times as a list drifting from its source of truth;
  * and only then add to the module, which is now small enough to verify a mechanism in.

**Eight mechanisms verified on their own inputs** before any of this was wired:

    axis decentring   the R-B at the frame's centre rises 0.09135 -> 0.19103 as the axis moves out
    chromatic         a position difference: R-G centroid -1.02 px at axis_offset 0.5
    longitudinal      a width difference with no position change: R/G 2.43, B/G 2.71, centroid 0.0000
    field curvature   centre/edge sharpness ratio 1.07 -> 1.16 -> 1.43
    coma              outward, one-sided, growing 0.67 -> 3.28 -> 8.78  (the sign was measured, not reasoned)
    astigmatism       directional and it flips: upper frame 1.162, lower frame 0.858
    spherical         symmetric and brightness-dependent: bright dot 205 -> 317, dim dot unchanged
    distortion        an off-axis edge moves by the sign of the parameter: -0.5 -> +1 px, +0.5 -> -2 px

Defaults are 0.0 for all five new parameters, so no preset moves until they are raised.

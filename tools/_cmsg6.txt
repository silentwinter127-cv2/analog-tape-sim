add connector degradation: moisture and corrosion at the physical interfaces

A connector is not a pipe. It is a node with an impedance, a contact resistance and a
shield, and when those change the *kind* of damage depends on what the interface was
carrying. That is why this is one module with four different behaviours rather than one
"damage" control, and why the artefacts should be recognisable as coming from a specific
junction in a specific kind of chain.

**HDMI** carries a high-speed differential stream. Corrosion raises the contact impedance
and the eye closes, so a bit is right or wrong and there is no "slight snow" state: the
artefacts are macroblock errors until correction fails, and then the link drops and reports
no signal. Measured, the bit error rate runs 0 to 0.93 across the ranges and the link
fails outright past the threshold.

**AV / RCA** carries baseband composite with the ground on the shell, which gives two
independent mechanisms. A high-resistance shell is a ground-loop path, so mains hum appears
as a bright bar rolling at the difference between mains and field rate -- the roll is the
whole character of the artefact. A raised centre-pin impedance reflects, and a reflection is
a delayed copy of the picture laid over itself: a ghost, not a blur.

**S-Video** splits luma and chroma onto adjacent pins, so moisture between them leaks each
into the other. The result is coloured fringes sitting exactly on edges rather than a colour
cast, because luma's sharp transitions appear in the chroma channel and vice versa. This is
the one that most convincingly reads as a bad tape while originating in the connector.

**RF** carries a modulated carrier, so damage alters the channel the picture travels in. The
reflection interferes with the forward wave, and whether that adds or cancels depends on
electrical length, so the damage is *frequency selective* rather than a uniform fade.
Corrosion loss also rises with frequency, so the chroma subcarrier goes before detail. And
a wet oxide layer can rectify, generating harmonics -- interference on *other* channels,
produced at the joint rather than received from outside.

**Two defects found by tracing, not by reading.**

HDMI and S-Video reported *exactly* zero effect while AV and RF worked. They were modifying
the picture; the player stage rebuilds its output from the composite plane, so a change to
the rgb buffer was discarded one stage later -- measured, the difference went from 0.037383
after the connector to 0.000000 after the player. The analogue connectors only appeared to
work because they happened to modify the composite. The stage now rebuilds one coherent
signal and encodes both views from it, because two representations that can disagree will
eventually disagree. This is the seventh instance in this project of a change to one
representation being annulled by a later stage reading the other.

The impedance model had the corrosion term at 0.55 of nominal, which on a 100-ohm
differential pair added 55 ohms and swamped the moisture term entirely -- so a soaked HDMI
connector came out at 130 ohms and the model said moisture *raised* the impedance of a
differential pair, which is backwards. At 0.35 the two mechanisms act in their physical
directions, and they can partly cancel, which is real: a wet-and-rusted joint can land
closer to nominal than a merely wet one.

A third: the HDMI sensitivity was set so the error rate peaked at 0.23, below the
link-failure threshold, making the "digital link drops outright" behaviour unreachable at
any setting. A control whose extreme cannot reach the state it exists to show is the same
defect as a control whose whole range is invisible.

**Deliberately not attributed to chemistry.** "Rust" and "moisture" are what the user sets;
the model commits to the electrical consequence and the artefact that follows. Naming an
oxide would be a guess dressed as a finding, and the same picture can come from several
causes.

Also added: the README now opens with the rule this project keeps relearning -- suspect the
instrument before changing a mechanism, with the six cases that taught it.

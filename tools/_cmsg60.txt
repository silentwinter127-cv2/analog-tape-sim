roll back the ring bokeh: three constructions, three measurements, one geometric fact

**The mechanism is real and it is not shipped.** Three constructions were tried and each was measured:

    wide_blur - narrow_blur        mostly negative -- a hole with a faint rim.  The centre fell by 0.125 and the
                                   ring's radius rose by 0.0003: it dimmed the highlight and made no ring
    an annulus weighted by local   nothing.  The detail of a defocused dot is a few pixels wide and one wide blur
    detail                         flattens it before an annulus can be taken; the rise was 0.00015
    narrow_blur - wide_blur, its   raises the **centre** by up to 0.33, because the difference of two blurs of the
    positive part                  same blob peaks at the blob's middle.  The largest rise was at r = 0 every time

**The third failure is a fact rather than a bug, and it is the one worth keeping:** a radial structure cannot be
obtained by convolving a compact feature, because the convolution of a blob is a blob. A ring has to be built
explicitly around each feature's own position and radius, which is per-feature work and belongs in its own design
rather than being improvised at the end of a long pass.

**Three attempts that each failed on a measurement are not a reason for a fourth.** The mechanism is rolled back
rather than shipped in a state where it dims highlights, and what each attempt measured is recorded so the next one
starts from the geometry instead of rediscovering it.

**And `flare_blades` was checked, because a note from an earlier turn called it inert and reading the code said
otherwise.** The reading was right and the note was wrong: `_lens_flare` builds an iris polygon and one ray per
blade, and `lens_flare` measurably acts -- ink beyond a point source's core rises 465 -> 811. **But `flare_blades`
barely changes it**: 6 blades against 8 gives 811 against 877, an 8% difference, and all 24 angular sectors carry
ink at both settings, so the rays are not distinguishable. So the parameter is wired and its effect is too weak to
read as a starburst, which is a different fault from an inert one and is not diagnosed.

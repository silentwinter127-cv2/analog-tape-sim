settle what the banding is: chroma-specific, real, and measured the right way

**The measurement problem, finally removed.** Six hypotheses were examined and every one of them was compared against
something incomparable: the encoder's own ripple, a ramp that a per-row displacement legitimately changes, or a
perfectly flat field. **A flat field is not a floor** -- a real photograph has scene structure, so the ratio to it is
meaningless and the multipliers it produced (2.5 million times) are meaningless with it.

**The criterion that works is the chain's own before-and-after**, on the same picture, split into luma and chroma:

                            row-to-row row-mean variation
    the source              luma 0.002631      R-B 0.003417
    through the neutral     luma 0.002569      R-B 0.011570
    chain (display off)
                            -2%, unchanged     3.4x

**So the banding is real, and it is chroma-specific.** The luma path is **unchanged** by the chain -- 0.002631 to
0.002569 -- while R-B rises by a factor of 3.4. That is the first statement in this whole investigation where both
sides are measured the same way, on the same subject, with nothing entangled.

**And it narrows the fault sharply, in combination with what was established last turn:** the chroma recovery is
**accurate for constant chroma** -- a designed constant I returns within 0.1%, ripple 0.000654 at I = 0.30. So:

    constant chroma through the chain        accurate to 0.1%
    spatially varying chroma through it      R-B row-to-row rises 3.4x

**The fault is in how the chain handles chroma that varies across the picture, not chroma as such.** That is
consistent with every result gathered -- the constant-signal tests passing, the real-picture tests failing, and the
five refuted hypotheses each removing a piece that a constant signal does not exercise.

**And the source's own figures are worth recording**, because they bound what can be asked of the chain rather than
blaming it:

    the file at its native 4363x3279     luma 0.000974     R-B 0.001005
    the same downsampled to 320x240      luma 0.002631     R-B 0.003417

so **the working resolution's own resampling already multiplies the statistic by 2.7x on luma and 3.4x on chroma**
before any stage acts. Part of what was being attributed to the chain is the input path's cost.

**Nothing changed**: `signal.py`, `player.py` and `standards.py` are byte-identical to their committed state, and
163 checks pass. The instruments are kept, and this record corrects the flat-field comparison they were being read
against.

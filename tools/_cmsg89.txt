**The methodological correction, which was the point of the turn.** The previous reproduction used a frame from the
DSC-W210 clip -- a CCD camera's **output** -- which stacks the model's artefacts on a camera's. That is the mistake
corrected earlier when the TRV310E photographs were being used as inputs, and it was made again. The input is now a
**raw photograph** and the model is the only processing in the chain.

**And the tension it resolves, which had blocked several attempts.** A raw photograph of a scene with no saturated
pixels gives every highlight mechanism nothing to act on -- the CC0 snow scene measures `blown 0.0000` and the
mechanisms are correctly inert on it. **A blown highlight is not a property of a picture being "good"; it is what a
light source looks like when the exposure is set for the scene around it.** So what the input needs is a raw
photograph that **contains a light source**, and that is measured rather than assumed:

    raw_lamp_snow.jpg    3840x2603   blown 0.00481   glow 0.02853   mean 0.2075   CC BY-SA 4.0
    raw_lamp_arena.jpg   3840x2560   blown 0.00977   glow 0.02068   mean 0.0946   CC BY-SA 4.0   <- used
    raw_lights_road.jpg  3456x5184   blown 0.00140   glow 0.00516   mean 0.2200   CC BY-SA 4.0
    raw_siena.jpg        3840x2560   blown 0.00652   glow 0.02272   mean 0.2284   CC BY 4.0

**One correction was needed after the first raw render, and it is a scale effect worth recording.** The control came
out clean and natural -- a white wall, an orange reflection, a railing -- and the effect put strong red-green-blue
fringing across the whole frame. The cause is arithmetic: **lateral chromatic aberration is radial and defined in
pixels**, and its coefficient had been chosen on 320-pixel test material where full strength displaces an edge by
about 2.7 px. **At 3840 px the same coefficient displaces it by more than fourteen.** So the aberrations were roughly
five times too strong for this material while the light mechanisms -- halation, the charge smear, the flare, the
overflow ring -- are per-unit-area and do not scale with the frame. **The two groups are now set separately**, and
the correction is visible as a number:

    fringing (mean |R-B|)   over-driven 0.02769   dialled 0.01769   control 0.01614
                            i.e. 1.72x the control, reduced to 1.10x

**The effect on the raw frame, against its own control:**

                        control     effect
    mean luma           0.05605     0.06016     +7.3%
    blown (>0.97)       0.01068     0.01274     +19%
    glow  (>0.85)       0.02097     0.02565     +22%
    fringing            0.01614     0.01769     +1.10x

**The settings file** `tools/ntscrs_ref/repro-highlight.json` carries 24 parameters across 6 layers with a fixed seed
and the NTSC standard: a digital-component path and a linear display, the tape's head-switching band off because the
material is a modern camera's, the chroma crosstalk terms at zero because the scene has no chroma detail to misplace,
and the nine highlight mechanisms split into the two groups above -- `halation 0.90`, `ccd_smear 0.85`,
`flare_defocus 1.00`, `lens_flare 0.50`, `spike_spread 0.85`, `overflow_drain 0.35` for the light behaviour, and
`chroma_aberration 0.20`, `longitudinal_ca 0.12`, `axis_offset 0.20` for the aberrations that scale with the frame.

Nothing in the model was changed. 163 checks pass, selftest clean.

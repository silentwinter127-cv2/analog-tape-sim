**Three specific things the numbers say, and none of them is what I had assumed.**

**The near field is warm, not cool.** At +60 and +100 the reference runs to **B-R -0.51** -- a strong orange -- while
the render reaches only -0.034. **The glow immediately under a lamp is a warm halo, and that is a different mechanism
from a cool streak**: it is the light of the source scattered in the air and in the lens, which carries the source's
own colour, whereas the earlier work has been adding a *cool* trail.

**The cool shift is far down and weak in the render.** At +180 the reference is at **+0.226** and the render at

and the render does not have it in either direction.

**What that means for the model, stated as a requirement rather than a guess.** A warm halo whose colour follows the
source and whose strength falls with distance, plus a cool shift that grows *further* down rather than immediately,

something any existing parameter addresses** -- `halation` adds a glow but its tint is fixed and cool, and
`lens_flare`'s tint is a coating colour rather than the source's. **So this is a mechanism that is missing, not a
value to be tuned**, and the evidence for that is the profile pair above rather than an impression.

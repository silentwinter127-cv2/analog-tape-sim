add per-line time-base error, and fix line_jitter being invisible

Two mechanisms on the horizontal axis, and a bug found while measuring them.

**row_timebase (new).** The existing timeline supplies a per-line displacement that
oscillates *within* a frame. What was missing is the other axis: a term that wanders
*across* frames. This field carries a random walk and a slow drift, so the fault
pattern migrates instead of returning to the same shape on every pass. Measured at
amount 0.8, consecutive frames differ by 5.2 px.

Luma and chroma are displaced by the *same* amount, and that is deliberate rather
than an implementation detail: a sampling-phase error moves the whole line, so
shifting the chroma carrier separately would invent a colour shift that no transport
produces. Measured, luma and chroma displacements differ by 0.05 px.

**line_jitter was invisible.** It scaled the per-line phase by unit_px * 5.0, and
unit_px = 0.045 * W / 720 is 0.0225 at a 360-wide preview -- so line_jitter = 1.0,
the top of its range, moved lines by 0.09 px. The measured standard deviation of the
timeline's per-line phase was 0.045 px, three orders of magnitude below one pixel.
A control whose entire range is under a pixel reads as a broken control, which is how
it was reported. Amplitudes are now expressed in pixels of the current render and the
same measurement gives 0.94 px.

**A correction to my own reasoning.** I wrote in the new docstring that the timeline
was uncorrelated line-to-line and that the new field was the correlated one.
Measurement said the opposite: the timeline's within-frame lag-1 correlation is about
+0.95, so it is already smooth along the picture, while this field is re-drawn per
frame and migrates. The docstring now records the measured behaviour and states that
both are retained because they are different behaviours, not two names for one.

**Measurement method mattered twice here.** The first attempt measured per-line
displacement on a signal that varied only along x, so a horizontal shift was not a
shift of anything measurable and every mechanism reported 0.00 px; measuring a
horizontal displacement needs vertical structure. The second attempt compared
mechanism-on against the original signal rather than against a mechanism-off run, so
every other tape mechanism was mixed in.

Verified: 41 checks pass, 13 presets render. curving_stripes from the same feedback
is still outstanding.

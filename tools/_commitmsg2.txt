add curving stripes, measured against a quantity that can tell them apart

The last of the three properties in the feedback. Building it required settling what
"curving" means as a measurement first, and that changed what the mechanism had to be.

**Displacement cannot bend a stripe, and this was measured, not assumed.** A grating
of known pitch was demodulated to recover its phase field, and the *second* derivative
was taken: a straight but slanted stripe has a nonzero first derivative and zero
second, so the second derivative is what separates "curved" from "tilted". Measured:

    intact grating      within-row 0.00001   across-row 0.00014
    slanted grating     within-row 0.00001   across-row 0.00018   (correctly not curved)
    row_timebase 1.0    within-row 0.00001   across-row 0.54927   (3990x)

The existing per-line time-base mechanism moves the *across-row* figure by a factor of
3990 while leaving the within-row figure at the floor. A first version of the check
used the across-row figure and would have accepted any displacement mechanism as
"curving". A row shift moves content sideways; it does not change the spatial
frequency, so a straight stripe stays straight.

**What bends a stripe is a beat.** If a residual carrier at a slightly different
frequency is present -- bias or audio subcarrier leaking into the video path, a
neighbouring channel, a clock not synchronous with the one the picture was recorded
with -- the difference frequency produces a slow envelope and the relative phase
drifts across the frame. The fringes curve because the phase difference is a function
of position. Implemented as luma-only, since it is an interference product in the
video path; giving it colour would be a separate claim.

Measured with the mechanism on:

    stripe_moire 0.3        within-row 0.00152  (117x baseline)
    stripe_moire 0.6        within-row 0.00301  (231x baseline)
    row_timebase 1.0        within-row 0.00001  (unchanged -- the distinction holds)
    stripe_curve 0 -> 0.8   across-row 0.00010 -> 0.00232
    pattern across frames   phase moves 0.1403 rad

**A check of mine was wrong, not the mechanism.** I first asserted that stripe_curve
changes the *within-row* curvature and it reported failure. The control actually
governs how the fringe *family* bends down the picture -- the across-row figure -- and
that is now what is asserted. Recording it because the failure looked like a broken
parameter and was a broken expectation.

44 checks pass, 14 presets render. The three properties from the feedback -- device
specificity, temporal change, and whole-line structure -- now each have a mechanism
and a measurement behind them.

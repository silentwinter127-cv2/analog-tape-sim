fix the MPEG-1 branch being unreachable, then mis-scaled by 100x

Two defects, found by tracing the high-frequency figure through each stage rather than
by reasoning about the code.

**The branch never ran.** `codec_kind = 2` selected MPEG-1 correctly, and `apply()` then
returned its input untouched because the guard was `if not self.enabled` -- and `enabled`
came only from `dv_enabled`, which a preset selecting MPEG-1 has no reason to set.
Traced, `meta["format"]` stayed "composite" through the codec stage. Fifth defect of this
shape in the project, and the same one every time: a feature selected correctly, then
annulled by a guard that consults a different variable.

**Then the quantiser was wrong by a factor of about a hundred.** The scale had been set
from a published figure and validated against an isolated test whose input amplitude
happened to suit it. Measured on the real coefficients: DC of order 1.3 and AC averaging
0.0094, against a step of 0.5 to 5.2 -- fifty times the mean AC coefficient, so **every
AC coefficient rounded to zero** and the output was a mosaic of DC values. Through the
chain that *raised* high frequency by 257% where the format lowers it, and the preset
scored a residual of 2.292 against the VCD reference, ranking it last rather than first.

The scale is now derived from the measured coefficient magnitudes (step 0.005 to 0.052,
so a typical AC coefficient sits at about 0.8 of a step -- at the rounding threshold,
which is what a coarse quantiser is). Measured:

    codec alone, reference frame   hf 0.05127 -> 0.05596, error 0.01433
    the preset through the chain   residual 2.292 -> 0.111
    its high frequency             1.362 -> 0.041 (reference 0.051)

So the VCD preset now sits second of sixteen presets by residual, behind `minidv_clean`
at 0.075. It is no longer last, and its high-frequency figure is *below* the reference
rather than thirty times above it -- the format's softness is present.

Four checks added: that the matrices and zigzag are the specified ones, that the branch
is reachable, that it is lossy with a working quality control, and that it does not
collapse into a mosaic. The last asserts a ceiling of 1.25x rather than a decrease,
because block edges at the 8x8 grid add a few percent of high frequency legitimately; the
failure it exists to catch measured +2950%.

Also worth recording, because three attempts were spent on it: the check had to be moved
to drive the codec directly. Measuring through the whole chain put the "before"
high-frequency figure at 0.00000, the format stage having band-limited the signal long
before the codec saw it, so the assertion could not fail either way. Two earlier
insertions of this check also failed on mechanics rather than substance -- appended to the
end of the file it was defined after the call (NameError), and located by a body fragment
it matched the wrong function and overwrote `check_fingerprint_ordering`. Boundaries
found with `ast` are what fixed that.

51 checks pass, 15 presets render.

Still open, unchanged: the row-time-base mechanism does not reproduce the real 8.8x
reduction a corrector gives, and the hue-spread gap has not been investigated.
